About CyberIndex & Evaluation Methodology

CyberIndex is an independent analytical project dedicated to reviewing and rating enterprise-grade cybersecurity providers, penetration testing companies, and PTaaS platforms.

Our Ranking Criteria

We do not accept paid placements. Every vendor is scored against strict quantitative and qualitative factors:

1. Manual vs. Automated Ratio: We penalize vendors relying heavily on off-the-shelf automated scanners without deep manual validation.

2. Reporting Quality: Presence of functional Proof-of-Concepts (PoC) and actionable remediation guidelines.
