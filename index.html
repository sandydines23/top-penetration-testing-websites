Frequently Asked Questions

How much does an enterprise penetration test cost? +

Enterprise manual penetration tests range from $10,000 to $50,000+ depending on the scope, number of API endpoints, business logic complexity, and compliance demands.

What is the difference between PTaaS and traditional pentesting? +

Traditional pentests yield a static annual PDF report at a single point in time. PTaaS (Pentest-as-a-Service) integrates continuous testing cycles alongside development pipelines, reducing time-to-remediation.

How long does a standard manual security audit take? +

Active testing usually takes between 2 to 4 weeks depending on the perimeter scale, followed by 1 week for report compilation and PoC verification.

Do these platforms include re-testing after patching? +

Most tier-1 providers include a complimentary re-test window within 30 to 45 days after the initial report submission to ensure vulnerabilities were fixed properly.
