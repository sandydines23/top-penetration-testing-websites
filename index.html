Frequently Asked Questions

What is the average cost of a penetration test in 2026?

Standard web application or network pentests range from $5,000 to $25,000 depending on scope, target complexity, and manual testing hours.

Why is manual testing superior to automated scanners?

Automated scanners miss complex business logic flaws, authorization bypasses, and multi-step exploit chains that human security engineers identify.
