Frequently Asked Questions

What is the average cost of a penetration test in 2026?

Standard web application or network pentests typically range from $5,000 to $25,000 depending on the scope, number of targets, and depth of manual testing required. Enterprise red team operations can range from $30,000 to $100,000+.

What certifications should a reputable pentest firm hold?

Look for offensive certifications such as OSCP (Offensive Security Certified Professional), CREST CRT/CCT, GXPN, or OSWE for individual engineers, along with corporate CREST accreditation or ISO 27001 certifications.
